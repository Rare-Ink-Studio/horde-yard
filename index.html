HORDE Yard

Your XRPL wallet becomes a living zombie world.

HORDE Yard is a creepy cozy collector game where players grow an undead yard by earning Horde Points. More points unlock zombies, props, rare visitors, boss zombies, events, and future rewards.

This first version is a playable demo. Use the buttons to test how holding, collecting, adding liquidity, and participating in events will grow the yard in the full XRPL version.

How to Play

  1. Connect your Xaman wallet. Today this is a demo button.
  2. Go to Play. This opens your HORDE Yard.
  3. Earn Horde Points. Use the demo buttons to test upgrades.
  4. Unlock the yard. More points bring zombies, props, rare visitors, and bosses.
  5. Share your yard. Screenshot it or copy the share text.

The Mission

Build the biggest, weirdest, most infected HORDE Yard.

How You Grow

In the full version, your wallet activity will create Horde Points through holding HORDE, holding NFTs, adding liquidity, trading, and joining events.

Why It Matters

HORDE becomes more than a token. It becomes a community game layer with status, rewards, events, and collector identity.
